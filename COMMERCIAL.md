# Commercial licensing

LANcast is available under two licences, and you choose which one you use it
under.

## The open licence — AGPL-3.0

The default. Run it, study it, change it, share it. If you distribute a modified
version, or run one as a service other people use over a network, you must offer
those people the source of your version under the same terms. See
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

For anyone self-hosting LANcast for themselves, their household or their
friends, **this is the licence you want and there is nothing to buy**. That is
the case LANcast was built for.

## The commercial licence

If you want to build on LANcast in a product whose source you do not intend to
publish — embedding it in an appliance, shipping it inside a closed
application, or offering a hosted service without releasing your changes — the
AGPL's obligations do not suit you, and a commercial licence is available
instead.

This is possible because the copyright is undivided: LANcast has a single
author, so its terms can be offered twice. There is no contributor whose
agreement would have to be collected first.

**Contact:** open an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/Conqueror-Mod/LANcast) titled *Commercial
licence enquiry*, and terms will be discussed privately from there.

## Contributing

Because the dual arrangement depends on undivided copyright, contributions
require a contributor licence agreement before they can be merged. This is not
scepticism about the contribution; it is the one thing that keeps the commercial
option available at all, and it is lost permanently the first time a patch lands
without one.

Bug reports, reproductions and discussion need no agreement of any kind, and are
worth as much.
