The same two controls straight out of butterchurn (browser/render.html, no pass, 120 frames):
  strobe: mean luminance 0.508, 59 of 120 frames black -- a full-field strobe at ~8 Hz
  amber:  mean luminance 0.556, a solid amber field

So the preset's comp shader drew a full-field 8 Hz strobe and a solid gold field, and what reached the
screen was a slow grey and a red-orange field. The pass runs after preset shaders.

Why that is structural, not luck (web/src/playback/MilkdropCanvas.tsx): butterchurn renders warp and
comp into its own WebGL2 context and copies the result into a 2D canvas that is never on the page;
milkdropPass.frame() uploads that canvas as a texture into a second WebGL2 context, which owns the
visible canvas. A preset's shaders run only in the first context and cannot reach the second.
No console errors during the session.

EEL at all; for the two real presets a grouping difference would change what they draw, not whether
the pass applies to it. browser/render.html loads web/vendor/butterchurn directly, patched.
