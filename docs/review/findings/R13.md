Two facts the findings depend on:
- `main.tsx` sets no default `staleTime`, so a query with none refetches when it remounts. A stale-after-write bug therefore needs either a mounted list or a list with its own long `staleTime`. The long ones that matter here are `["memories"]` (15 min), `["home-shelves"]` and `["continue","suggestion"]` (60 s), and `["recent-photos"]` and `["recently-added"]` (30 s).
- The server caps `/api/items` at `limit` 500 and defaults it to **100** when no limit is sent (`internal/store/store.go:1846`).

## R13-1 A container's page lists only its first 100 children

- **Severity:** S2
- **Class:** other
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/api/hooks.ts:1287` (`useChildren`)
- **Failure:** Open a photo folder (gallery) holding 250 photos, or a season with more than 100 episodes. The page shows 100. The count line says "100 photos", because `Detail.tsx:898` counts the array it got. The photo banner and viewer page through 100. On a season, "Play all" queues the first 100 episodes (`playableChildren`, `Detail.tsx:721`), and the watched action that `watchedAction(item, children, …)` builds marks only those 100. Nothing on screen says the list was cut short.
- **Evidence:**
  ```ts
  // hooks.ts:1287
  queryKey: ["children", parentID, sort ?? ""],
  queryFn: ({ signal }) =>
    apiGet<ItemsPage>(`/api/items?parent_id=${parentID}` + (sort ? `&sort=${sort}` : ""), signal)
      .then((r) => r.items),          // `total` is thrown away
  ```
  ```go
  // internal/store/store.go:1845
  limit := f.Limit
  if limit <= 0 || limit > 500 { limit = 100 }
  ```
  The same file already knows about this cap. `fetchDescendantIDs` (`hooks.ts:3367`) carries a comment about it ("a container with more than a hundred children would produce a Play all that … quietly left out the rest. Rare in a season and entirely ordinary in a gallery") and pages for exactly that reason. `useChildren`, which feeds the page itself, does not page.
- **Fix sketch:** Page `useChildren` to `total` the way `fetchDescendantIDs` does (500 per request), or make it an infinite query and let `Detail` load more. Keep the key a child of `["children", parentID]` so existing invalidations still reach it.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED. The only caller is `Detail.tsx:511`, which renders the gallery banner, the children grid, the count label and "Play all" from this array. No second fetch fills in the rest. Books are not affected: their parts reach the player through `/api/items/{id}/book`, and `Detail.tsx:1452` hides the children section for a book.
- **Triage:** Fixed and merged in #1094.

## R13-2 Clearing watch history leaves Home's "On this day" showing what you cleared

- **Severity:** S2
- **Class:** C6
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/api/hooks.ts:4632` (`useResetHistory`)
- **Failure:** In Settings → Privacy, clear finished history, then go Home. The "On this day" panel keeps the "watched" cards (films you finished on this date in earlier years) for up to 15 minutes. Those cards come from the `viewing` rows the reset just deleted (`FinishedOnThisDay`, `internal/store/finishedmemories.go:44`). The panel reads `["memories", 20]`, which has `staleTime: 15 * 60_000`, and the reset does not invalidate that key, so the remount serves the cached answer. The same holds for the year-in-review card (`["year-in-review", …]`, 60 s) and for `["to-rate"]` if it is mounted. For a control called "clear history", showing the history back is the worst kind of stale.
- **Evidence:** The reset invalidates `history-count, profile, items, continue, recently-added, review, children` (`hooks.ts:4644`). `memories`, `year-in-review` and `to-rate` are missing. Nothing in `web/src` invalidates `["memories"]` at all (grep: no `invalidateQueries` names it, and no `topicKeys` row does).
- **Fix sketch:** Add `memories`, `year-in-review` and `to-rate` to the reset's list. Consider adding `memories` to `invalidateWatchState` too, so marking a finished film unwatched is covered.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED. `useResetHistory` is called from `screens/settings/privacy.tsx`, and Home is not mounted while you are there. On return, `useMemories(20)` is still inside its 15-minute window.
- **Triage:** Fixed and merged in #1094.

## R13-3 Home's photo shelves ignore "Mark sensitive" and "Remove"

- **Severity:** S2
- **Class:** C6
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/api/hooks.ts:582` (`useSetSensitive`), `web/src/api/hooks.ts:1361` (`useDeleteItem`)
- **Failure:** Mark a photo sensitive from its tile menu, then go Home. The photo is drawn **unblurred** in "On this day" for up to 15 minutes and in New this week's photo row for up to 30 seconds. Both draw through `PosterTile`, whose `useObscured(item)` reads `item.sensitive` from the cached list. The blur is the whole point of ADR 0051, and this gap fails at exactly the moment it is needed. Removing a photo (or any title) has the same gap: the removed photo stays on both shelves for the same windows. A collection page's grid (`["collection-members", id]`) also keeps the old thumbnail after the mark, while you are looking at it.
- **Evidence:** `useSetSensitive` invalidates `item, items, items-infinite, children, recently-added, search` (`hooks.ts:588`). `["items-infinite"]` matches no query: the grid's key became `["items","infinite",…]` (`hooks.ts:3082`), so that line is dead and harmless. `useDeleteItem` invalidates the list at `hooks.ts:1374`. Neither reaches `["recent-photos", …]` (`hooks.ts:1740`) or `["memories", …]` (`hooks.ts:1726`). `useSetSensitive` also misses `collection-members` and `playlist-entries`. Home builds the panels from those two keys (`screens/Home.tsx:130–131`, `196`, `205`) and renders them through `PosterTile` (`components/BridgePanels.tsx:165`).
- **Fix sketch:** Add `recent-photos` and `memories` to both mutations, and `collection-members` and `playlist-entries` to `useSetSensitive`. Then drop the dead `items-infinite` line.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED. The server event stream does not cover this: removing or marking an item publishes no topic (only scans and enrichment publish `items`, `internal/serverapp/main.go:990`, `1027`), and `memories` has no `topicKeys` row in any case.
- **Triage:** Fixed and merged in #1094.

## R13-4 Comparing one episode's collision shows another episode's files

- **Severity:** S2
- **Class:** C7
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/api/types.ts:416` (hand-written `Collision`), `web/src/api/hooks.ts:1538` (`useCompareCollision`); used at `web/src/components/Collisions.tsx:139–145`
- **Failure:** A season ripped twice gives one collision per episode, and every one has the *show's* `external_id`. The spec says the work key is `(provider, external_id, season, episode)`. Press Compare on S01E05's card. The request asks `?compare=<show id>`, the server fingerprints every episode of the show (all of them match `compare == c.ExternalID`), and the card takes `data.collisions.find(c => c.external_id === collision.external_id)`. That is S01E01. The S01E05 card then lists **S01E01's files** with S01E01's same-bytes verdict, and an administrator deciding which copy to delete is reading the wrong pair. The cards' React key `${provider}:${external_id}` (`Collisions.tsx:291`) also collides for every episode of the show.
- **Evidence:** `types.ts` claims hand-written types exist only for live TV and `ApiError`. Yet it restates `Collision`/`CollisionMember` (lines 398–437), which the spec defines as `components["schemas"]["Collision"]` with `season?` and `episode?` (`schema.ts:8183`). The hand-written copy drops both, so no caller can tell episodes apart. Underneath sits a server-side contract break: `internal/api/collisions.go:70` builds its own `collision` struct without `season`/`episode`, so the handler never sends fields the spec documents (see Seen in passing).
- **Fix sketch:** Re-export `Collision` from `schema.ts`. Have the handler send `season`/`episode`. Have `useCompareCollision` key and match on the full work key, or on the member ids.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED by reading the handler and the card. Not reproduced on a live server. The trigger needs two episodes of one show that each collide, which a duplicated season folder produces.
- **Triage:** Fixed and merged in #1094.

## R13-5 Escape runs the screen's Back instead of closing the dialog that screen just opened

- **Severity:** S2
- **Class:** C12
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/focus/FocusController.tsx:416` (`useBackHandler`)
- **Failure:** On Search, Favourites/tags (`Marked`) or Collections, open a tile's menu and choose "Add to playlist" or "Remove". Press Escape, or Back on a remote. The dialog stays where it was, and the screen navigates away instead (to the previous page, to /profile, or to the library). The dialog's open state lives in the screen (`useItemActions`, `components/itemActions.tsx:219`). Those screens pass an inline `back`, so opening the dialog re-renders the screen and re-runs the screen's `useBackHandler` effect. React runs that effect's cleanup and then its setup *after* the new dialog's setup (children before parents), so the screen's handler is pushed back on top of the dialog's. The effect's cleanup depends on a function rebuilt every render, which is C12 in a new shape: it reorders a stack.
- **Evidence:** A proving test, run in this worktree and not committed (`web/src/focus/backOrder.r13.test.tsx`):
  ```tsx
  function Dialog({ onClose }: { onClose: () => void }) { useBackHandler(onClose); return <div>dialog</div>; }
  function Screen() {
    const [shown, setShown] = useState(false);
    open = () => setShown(true);
    const back = () => calls.push("screen back");      // inline, as in Marked/Collections/Search
    useBackHandler(back);
    return shown ? <Dialog onClose={() => { calls.push("dialog close"); setShown(false); }} /> : null;
  }
  // render, act(open), dispatch keydown Escape
  expect(calls).toEqual(["dialog close"]);
  ```
  ```
  - Expected  [ "dialog close" ]
  + Received  [ "screen back" ]
   Tests  1 failed (1)
  ```
  Inline handlers: `screens/Search.tsx:35` (`() => navigate(-1)`), `screens/Marked.tsx:62`, `screens/Collections.tsx:76`, `screens/Playlists.tsx:264`, `screens/RetroWebPlay.tsx:138`. `Detail.tsx:644` wraps its `back` in `useCallback`, which is why the detail page does not show this.
- **Fix sketch:** Make the hook independent of identity. Push once on mount with a stable wrapper that calls `fnRef.current`, and keep the ref current each render. A handler's place in the stack is then fixed by when the component mounted, not by when it last rendered.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED by the test above. A dialog reached through any screen whose back handler is inline is affected. A future screen with an inline handler will hit the same thing, which is why the fix belongs in the hook and not in the screens.
- **Triage:** Fixed and merged in #1094.

## R13-6 A list that the server changes is not refreshed while you are looking at it, unless it is one of eight

- **Severity:** S3
- **Class:** C6
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/api/hooks.ts:921` (`workFinishedKeys`), `web/src/api/serverEvents.ts:36` (`topicKeys.items`)
- **Failure:** A scan marks a film missing, or enrichment retitles one, while you are on a collection's page, a playlist, a search result, the photo timeline or the duplicates view, or while Home is showing "On this day". They do not invalidate `collection-members`, `playlist-entries`, `search`, `timeline`, `duplicates`, `memories` or `item-collections`.

- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED on the mechanism. The damage is bounded: each of these screens refetches on remount (default staleTime 0), except `memories`, which waits up to 15 minutes.

## R13-7 Reconnecting the event stream does not mark cached screens stale

- **Severity:** S3
- **Class:** C6
- **Confidence:** low
- **Where:** `web/src/api/serverEvents.ts:159`
- **Failure:** The stream drops (a server restart for an update, a laptop asleep), and a scan or enrichment finishes during the gap. On reconnect only *active* queries are invalidated. A screen you then open that is still inside its `staleTime` is served the pre-gap answer without a refetch: Home's pinned shelves and suggestion (60 s), recently added (30 s), "On this day" (15 min). ADR 0079 §4 and the comment above the hook say "every reconnect refetches everything on screen". That holds for what is on screen; what is cached comes back stale.
- **Evidence:** `if (opened) void qc.invalidateQueries({ type: "active" });`. `type: "active"` both *marks* and refetches only the active queries, so inactive ones keep their freshness.
- **Fix sketch:** Call `qc.invalidateQueries()` with no filter. Its default `refetchType: "active"` still refetches only what is mounted, but it marks everything else stale for its next mount.
- **Verdict:** PLAUSIBLE. The window is the shorter of the gap and the query's `staleTime`, so in practice it is mostly `memories`. Recorded at low confidence for the verify stage.
- **Triage:** Before 1.0, wave F. Fixed and merged in #1151.

## R13-8 The download receipts list is shared by every account that signs in on the device

- **Severity:** S3
- **Class:** other
- **Confidence:** high
- **Where:** `web/src/lib/downloads.ts:52` (`useDownloads`, key `lancast:downloads` in localStorage)
- **Failure:** A parent downloads a film on the family laptop and signs out, and a child's account (with a rating ceiling, or without access to that library) signs in. Downloads lists the parent's titles, filenames, and series and episode lines, and Detail marks those items "downloaded". `forgetSession` (`hooks.ts:336`) clears the query cache and the sensitive-content acknowledgements, but not this.
- **Evidence:** `useDevice<Receipt[]>(DOWNLOADS_KEY, [])`. Nothing on sign-out calls `forgetDevice(DOWNLOADS_KEY)`, and the key is not per account.
- **Fix sketch:** Key the receipts by account id (`lancast:downloads:<user id>`). That keeps the per-device rule the file argues for while dropping the cross-account leak.
- **Verdict:** CONFIRMED on the mechanism. Per-device storage is a stated design choice (`downloads.ts:16`), but the stated reason (the device that downloaded it has the file) does not cover showing the list to a different person. Triage decides whether this is a fault.
- **Triage:** Before 1.0, wave A. Fixed and merged in #1153.

Optimistic updates: there are three (`useGrantPresence`, `useSetHomePrefs`, `useSetReadingPrefs`), and each cancels in-flight reads first and rolls back in `onError`. `useBackgroundLibraryJob` and its siblings write a synthetic `active: true` into `["activity"]` and then invalidate it at once, so a failure there cannot stick.

**C7.** `types.ts` re-exports 133 shapes from `schema.ts`. The hand-written ones are `MatchState` (matches the server's five states), `ApiError`, the live-TV shapes (parked) and `Collision`/`CollisionMember` (R13-4). Account ids are typed `string` everywhere they appear in `hooks.ts` (users, invites, sessions, two-factor, pictures, user-libraries). Item and library ids are `number`. `schema.test.ts` guards the generated half.

**C11.** The slice has no `toISOString`, no `valueAsDate` and no `getUTC*`. `lib/when.ts` and `lib/resume.ts` build calendar days from local components, and `useMemories` takes the day from the server on purpose.

**C12.** Every `useEffect` with a cleanup in `web/src/lib` and `web/src/focus`, plus `useForgetEndedSession` and `useServerEvents` in `api`, was checked. One cleanup depends on an identity rebuilt every render: `useBackHandler`'s (R13-5). `HomePrefsCarryOver` names the `useMutation` result it ignores in its deps, has no cleanup, and is guarded by a ref. `useFocusable`'s `setSelect` effect re-runs per render with no cleanup, which is harmless.

**`client.ts` errors.** A 4xx or 5xx always throws `ApiFailure` with the server's code. A 204 returns null, and nothing reports a failed request as success. Mutations use the TanStack default `retry: 0`, so no non-idempotent request is retried. Queries retry once, including after a 401, which is harmless. A 401 on any *query* sends the window to sign-in through the `QueryCache` handler in `main.tsx`. A 401 on a *mutation* shows that screen's own error and nothing else, but `useActivity` polls every 8 s, so the window still reaches sign-in within seconds. That is not entered as a finding.

**Event stream.** Topics and keys are in `topicKeys`. A `CLOSED` stream (a 401 or 503) is reopened after 15 s, a stream the browser drops reconnects by itself, the cleanup stops both, and the stream is mounted only inside the signed-in app. What is stale after a gap is R13-7. What the topics never reach is R13-6.

**State across accounts.** `forgetSession` drops every cached query and the sensitive-content acknowledgements, and runs on sign-out and whenever the signed-in account changes. The device store (`lib/device.ts`) holds deliberately per-device settings: text size, big-screen mode, key bindings, the dock corner, tile size, and legacy home preferences that are carried to the account once. The one per-person record in it is the download receipts (R13-8).
