## Achievements while you play

- **Earn RetroAchievements in the desktop app.** Play a game in the LANcast window and its achievements unlock and count on retroachievements.org, as they do in other emulators. An unlock shows a pop-up over the game. The game menu has an **Achievements** page listing the set, with the ones you've unlocked first.
- **Off until you turn it on.** Connect your RetroAchievements account on your profile, then turn on **Earning while playing** with your RetroAchievements password. The password is used once and not kept: your LANcast server keeps the sign-in token RetroAchievements gives back, sealed, and the game never holds it. Turning it off deletes the token.
- **Everything goes through your LANcast server**, never from the app straight to RetroAchievements. If RetroAchievements can't be reached when you unlock something, your server keeps the unlock and sends it later, dated when you earned it. Your profile shows how many are waiting.
- **Progress is kept in save states.** An achievement you were partway through when you saved is still partway through when you load.
- **Casual only for now.** Hardcore needs RetroAchievements to approve LANcast first, which can't happen before February 2027. RetroAchievements may say LANcast is an unknown emulator; casual unlocks still count.
- **SNES games can't earn yet.** The SNES core LANcast uses doesn't let anything read the game's memory, so their achievements show as unsupported. Tested working on Nintendo 64; the other consoles haven't been tried with a real account yet.
- **Not in this release:** leaderboards. RetroAchievements sees what you're doing in a game (Rich Presence), which can't be turned off; LANcast doesn't show it.

## Friends' servers

- **A friend on another network can now reach your server.** If your server has a public name (**Settings › Remote access › Public certificate**), the invite it makes now starts with that name. Before, an invite carried only your computer's own addresses, so a friend whose network couldn't use your IPv6 had no way in, and the pairing showed their server, or yours, as not answering.
- **Friends who paired before need a fresh invite from you.** A server remembers the addresses from the invite it was given and never looks again. Send a new invite, and have them remove you and add it.
- This fixes your friends reaching you. For you to reach a server behind somebody else's router, that router still has to let LANcast in, or you both need a VPN such as Tailscale.

## Other fixes

- **A crash record says where, even for the hardest crashes.** One kind of crash wrote no summary line, so its record named nothing. It now names the place in LANcast it happened.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.2.exe`**.

**To earn achievements after an in-app update from 0.10.1 or earlier, run `LANcast-Setup-0.10.2.exe` once.** Achievements need a new file, `lancast-cheevos.dll`, beside LANcast, and an update from an older version only knows to bring the files that version knew about. Without it, games play as before and earn nothing. Updates from 0.10.2 on bring it themselves.

**This release changes the database**, so 0.10.1 can't be reinstalled over it afterwards. LANcast backs up the database before updating.
