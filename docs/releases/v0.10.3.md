## Browse music by album, track and genre

- **Show a music library as Artists, Albums or Tracks.** A switch at the start of the filter row. Albums and Tracks list every album or track in the library, not just the artists at the top.
- **Filter by genre and year.** Genre works on all three levels; an artist or album is in a genre when something under it is. Year and Decade work on Albums and Tracks. Albums can be sorted by year, and tracks by year or length.
- **Genres come from your files' tags, exactly as written.** A genre you edit in LANcast is kept, and a rescan won't change it back. "Rock" and "rock" count as one genre. A tag that holds several genres in one string, like `Indie / Alternative`, shows as that one genre; fix it in your tagger and the next scan picks it up.
- **Rescan your music library after updating.** Genres appear once the scan has re-read your files' tags. That first scan reads every track, so it takes longer than usual.
- **An artist's page lists all their tracks**, under their albums, with **Play all** and **Shuffle** for everything by them.
- **In Now Playing, the artist's name goes to the artist's page**, as the album name already went to the album. A compilation, or a track by "X feat. Y", leaves the name as plain text, since it isn't one artist's page.

## Smoother visualizers

- **The visualizers no longer jitter on a TV.** When the TV ran at a different refresh rate from your main monitor, the visualizer drew at the main monitor's pace and the TV showed some frames for longer than others. In the desktop app it now draws once per refresh of the screen it's on.
- **Shuffling a whole library no longer slows the visualizer.** A queue of thousands of tracks used to redraw itself several times a second.
- **Coming back from the mini-player keeps your shuffle.** It used to reshuffle the queue and start the count again from 1.
- **A photosensitivity warning** for the visualizers, in **This device › Player** and in the Visualizers settings. Search Settings for "seizure" to find it.

## Other changes

- **On a phone, the filter panel fits the screen.** It used to run off the right edge.
- **Privacy policy.** What LANcast sends outside your network, when, and what has to happen first. Linked from **Settings › About**.
- **Achievements:** your profile now says that hardcore mode isn't offered yet, and why. The hardcore rules are built but stay off until RetroAchievements approves LANcast, which can't happen before February 2027.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.3.exe`**. An in-app update from 0.10.2 also brings `lancast-cheevos.dll` if an earlier update left it out, so achievements work afterwards.

**This release changes the database**, so 0.10.2 can't be reinstalled over it afterwards. LANcast backs up the database before updating.
