## Pin your own shelves to Home

- **Pin to Home.** On any library, filter or re-sort the grid and **Pin to Home** appears beside Clear. Give the shelf a name (it starts as the filters you chose, like "Action · 1980s") and it appears on Home, below Rate what you've watched.
- **Your shelves, your order.** Each pinned shelf has a **⋯** menu at the end of its heading: **Rename**, **Move up**, **Move down** and **Unpin**. **All** opens the library with the same filters on.
- Up to 12 per account. A shelf with nothing in it today keeps its heading, so you can still unpin it.

## The spotlight remembers what you like

When the spotlight is on **Suggested** and you have nothing in progress, it now picks from what you've been finishing: "Because you've finished 3 Comedy titles lately", with a film or show in that genre you haven't started.

## On this day remembers what you finished

The **On this day** panel now shows films, episodes, audiobooks and comics you finished on today's date in an earlier year, alongside photographs, each marked **Watched**, **Listened** or **Read**. It uses only watch history with a real date, which LANcast began keeping on 30 September 2026, so this starts appearing on 30 September 2027.

## Fixes

- **Tag** and **Favourites** filters on a library now narrow the grid. Before, the filter showed but the grid ignored it.
- Finishing an audiobook or a comic now appears in your watch history.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.10.exe`**.

**This release changes the database**, to keep your pinned shelves. Once 0.10.10 has started, an older version can't open the database, so update forward rather than reinstalling 0.10.9.
