## Settings, sorted by what each one is about

Settings is now grouped by what a setting describes: **Your account** follows you to every device, **This device** stays on the screen in front of you, and **Server** is for whoever runs LANcast.

- **Your account › Home.** The spotlight, spoiler hiding, a switch for each built-in shelf (New this week, Rate what you've watched, Watchlist, Unwatched) and your pinned shelves, which you can move and unpin here too. These used to live on each device, so the first time you sign in on a device, its old choices are carried up to your account once.
- **Your account › Reading & listening.** The speed an audiobook starts at, a sleep timer that's set whenever a book starts, and how comics are laid out: one page or two facing pages, fitted to the screen or to its width. A book you've already set a speed on keeps its own.
- **Your account › Connected accounts.** Your RetroAchievements connection moved here from the profile. The profile still shows your progress.
- **This device.** The old Player pane is now four: Video, Music & Now Playing, Theater output and Remote play.
- **TV remote.** Every control in Settings can now be reached and changed with a remote.
- **Phones.** Settings opens on its list, then each pane full width, with a "‹ Settings" link back.

## Comics: two facing pages, and right to left

- **Two facing pages.** The cover sits alone, then pages pair as they were printed. Turn a phone on its side to get two.
- **Fit to width.** Up and Down scroll the page, and Space reads down it before turning.
- **Right to left, for manga.** Set it on a comics library (Settings › Libraries › Edit), and the left arrow, a tap on the left and the left-hand button all go forward. A spread puts the earlier page on the right. An admin can also set one series the other way from the reader's bar, and new issues in that folder follow it.

## Metadata in your language

- **Metadata language** (Settings › Metadata): film and show titles, descriptions and taglines are fetched from TMDB in any of 33 languages. Where TMDB has no translation, the English text is used, and files named in English still match.
- **Per library, too.** An anime library can be fetched in Japanese beside films in English (Settings › Libraries › Edit).
- **Nothing is rewritten until you ask.** After changing a language, each library offers a refresh with its count. It fetches the same titles again in the new language, keeps every match, and never touches anything you edited by hand.
- Trailers in the chosen language are preferred, with English ones as well.

## Your picture, and a new profile

- **Upload a picture of yourself.** Use Edit under your picture on the profile. Drag and zoom to frame it. LANcast keeps its own resized copy, never the file you uploaded, so location and camera details are gone. It shows in the rail, on People, in Settings › Users and in Watch Together. Admins can turn member uploads off, and can remove anyone's picture.
- **The profile has tabs:** Overview, History, Ratings & tags and Achievements.
- **Totals by kind.** Films and episodes, music, audiobooks and comics are counted separately, so an album on repeat no longer reads as hours of film. **Most played** lists what you've finished more than once.
- **One History** with a filter (All, Video, Music, Books, Comics), and a switch between each title once and every time. It replaces the separate full watch history page.
- **Books, comics and games** in progress and finished now appear on Overview.

## Storage

**Settings › Backup & storage** now shows what LANcast keeps on disk: the database, artwork, scrub-bar previews, backups, models, game saves and the rest. Each part shows its size, its share, and whether it could be fetched or made again if lost. Free space on the drive is shown too. It only reports; nothing on it deletes.

## Fixes

- Posters show again in your profile's history and ratings.
- **New this week** now leads the shelves under the spotlight, when there's anything new.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.11.exe`**.

**This release changes the database** four times, for your account's home and reading choices, pictures, and library options. Once 0.10.11 has started, an older version can't open the database, so update forward rather than reinstalling 0.10.10.
