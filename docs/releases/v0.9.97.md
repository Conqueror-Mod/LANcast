## Fixes

- **After restoring a backup, LANcast shows what you restored.** Signing back in after a restore could show a library under its old name until you opened another screen. The page now reloads as soon as the server is back.
- **Signing in after being signed out elsewhere starts clean.** When a session ended some other way than pressing Sign out (a restore, a password change, **Sign out every other device**, or simply expiring), the sign-in screen appeared over what that account had open, and whoever signed in next could briefly see it. Now LANcast forgets it, the same way Sign out always has.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.97.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database.
