## Fixes

- **The close button works while a film plays.** With a film in the full player, the window's ✕ did nothing; it only worked once the film was docked. Now it closes from either. The film stops and keeps your place.
- **Reopening after closing during a film.** Bringing LANcast back from the tray no longer lands on an empty player. You come back to where you were before the film. Music still keeps playing in the tray.
- **Play from start is remembered.** Starting a film over from its page now clears the old position right away, so Home shows it from the beginning too.
- **Taking things off Continue Watching.** The home spotlight now has the same menu as the shelf: right-click it, or press **More**. A film's or track's own page also offers **Remove from Continue Watching** while it's on the shelf.

## About, redesigned

- **Settings › About** now has a header, a table of facts about your installation, this release's notes as cards, and every component LANcast is built from in tables, with the full notice below.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.81.exe`**.
