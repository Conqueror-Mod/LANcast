## LANcast 1.0.3

Tidier browsing of a friend's library, and Download where you would look for it.

## Improved

- **A friend's collections have their own page.** Collections were mixed into the grid of a library someone shares with you, so its count included them: a library of 1,253 films read 1,440. The grid now shows films only, as your own does, and a **Collections** button beside the search lists their collections in one place. Searching their library still finds a collection by name.
- **Download is in the menus.** On a friend's film, Download has moved out from under the title to **More Options** at the right of the player's header. Right-clicking a friend's title now opens the same kind of menu as your own: **Play**, or **Open** for a show or collection, and **Download** where their share allows it.
- **Downloads list.** A file from a friend's server no longer shows its title twice.

## Updating

Use the in-app update (Settings, General), or run `LANcast-Setup-1.0.3.exe` over the top. The database is unchanged since 1.0.2, so going back to 1.0.2 is just reinstalling it. A friend's screen needs 1.0.3 for the Collections button and the menus; the server sharing with them needs it for the film count to drop the collections.
