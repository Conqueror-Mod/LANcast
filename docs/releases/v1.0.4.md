## LANcast 1.0.4

One fix to what comes after a film.

## Fixed

- **"Next in the collection" is the next film.** When a film in a collection ends, the page that follows now offers the film directly after it, even if you have seen that one before. It used to skip any later film already watched, so finishing Rush Hour offered Rush Hour 3 instead of Rush Hour 2. A film above your rating limit, or in a library hidden from you, is still skipped. The collection's own queue and the collection row on Continue Watching are unchanged: they still pick up from the first film you have not watched.

## Updating

Use the in-app update (Settings, General), or run `LANcast-Setup-1.0.4.exe` over the top. The database is unchanged since 1.0.2, so going back to 1.0.3 is just reinstalling it.
