## Collections

- **A collection shows what's missing.** Its page now lists the films in the franchise that aren't in your library, below the ones that are, with their posters and years. A film that's been announced but not released says "Coming 2027", or "Not yet released" if it has no date.
- **A collection's page says what it is.** Under the title: the years it spans, its total running time, how far through it you are, and "You have 3 of 5" when films are missing.
- **Collections have descriptions.** They come from TMDB. Administrators can write their own on the collection's page, for groupings TMDB doesn't describe, like the Marvel Cinematic Universe. A description you write is kept through every metadata refresh.
- **The Collections page shows what you've watched.** A collection you've finished gets the watched mark. One you've started shows a progress bar and "2 of 5 watched", or "Started" when you've begun one but not finished any.
- **Continue watching a collection's film carries on through the collection.** Before, resuming a film from Continue Watching played that film and stopped. Pressing Play on a film you haven't started still plays just that film.
- Existing collections fill in their films and descriptions by themselves, a few minutes after the update. You don't need to refresh anything.
- Accounts with a content rating limit aren't shown missing films. LANcast can't check the rating of a film it doesn't have.

## Retro games in the browser

- **Phones can play.** On a touch screen, on-screen controls appear for each console: d-pad, the console's own buttons, Start and Select. They step aside when a controller is connected.
- **Save states in the browser.** A Save states button opens three numbered slots. The game pauses while the panel is open. Each slot says whether its state came from this browser or the desktop app, and the browser only offers to load its own.

## Getting in touch

- **Report an issue** is in the side menu, and in More on a phone. It opens your own mail program with the version filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it.
- **Settings › About** names the developer and has a link to support LANcast on Ko-fi.

## Other servers

- **Invites are readable.** Your invite and any invite you paste show a short check code, the server's name, its fingerprint and its addresses. The check code changes completely if anything in the invite changes, so two people pairing can read theirs to each other.
- **Invites no longer include an address nobody else can reach.** On a computer with WSL or Hyper-V, the invite carried that virtual network's address too.

## Fixes

- **Shuffling the whole music library starts playing.** The first track used to fail with "The server refused to start playback", because the queue asked for every track at once and the browser gave up.
- **HEIC photos appear in the Timeline and On this day.** Their capture time wasn't being read. Existing ones are read again once, after the update.
- **Uploaded MilkDrop presets keep their own shaders**, instead of falling back to the default look. A preset whose shader can't be converted still works, with the defaults.
- **A library file that's a link to somewhere outside the library is no longer served.** Scans skip it and list it under the scan's issues.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.95.exe`**.

**This update changes the database.** Once it has run, 0.9.94 and earlier can't open your library. To go back, you'd need a backup taken before updating.
