## Visualizers

Now Playing can show the music instead of the album cover.

- **Choose under the cover:** Cover, Spectrum, Scope or Trails. LANcast remembers your choice on each device, and you can also set it in **Settings › Player**.
- **Spectrum** shows bars for each part of the sound, with caps that fall slowly. **Scope** draws the sound wave as a line. Both take their colours from the album cover.
- **Trails** are five moving patterns: **Tunnel**, **Spiral**, **Smoke**, **Bloom** and **Drift**. Each one redraws the sound wave over its own fading echoes and moves with the bass. Use **‹ ›** under the cover, or the **,** and **.** keys, to switch between them. Trails need a device with WebGL2; without it, Spectrum and Scope are still there.
- **Full screen:** press the **⛶** button or **V**. The controls fade away after a few seconds, the same as in a film, and come back when you move the mouse or press a key. **Escape** returns to Now Playing.
- **Calm** halves the movement and stops colour changes on the beat. Choose it in the playback settings, or in **Settings › Player**. If your device is set to reduce motion, visualizers are always calm.
- Visualizers stay gentle on purpose: the screen never flashes with the beat.
- The new keys can be changed in **Settings › Keyboard**, like the others.

## Fixes

- **Crossfades no longer go quiet after you turn on night mode mid-song.** Before, the next crossfade played at the volume slider's level twice over, so it came out too quiet.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.90.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.89 if you need to.
