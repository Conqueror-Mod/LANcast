## MilkDrop presets

You can now add **MilkDrop presets**, the visualizations made famous by Winamp, and play them under Now Playing beside LANcast's own.

- **Add them in Settings › Visualizers.** Admins can upload `.milk` files, or presets in butterchurn's JSON format, and remove them again. Everyone signed in to your server can play them.
- **Pick MilkDrop under the cover**, next to Cover, Spectrum, Scope and Trails. It appears once your server has at least one preset. Use **‹ ›**, or the **,** and **.** keys, to move between presets, and **V** for full screen.
- **Shaders aren't supported yet.** Many MilkDrop 2 presets come with shaders, and LANcast plays those presets with MilkDrop's default shaders, so some look plainer than intended. The settings page says which presets this affects.
- **Every preset is checked when it's uploaded**, and LANcast refuses any it can't read.
- **Presets never run as code in your browser.** Their equations are turned into a safe, sandboxed form, never into JavaScript.
- **LANcast keeps MilkDrop presets from flashing.** Many presets were made to strobe. LANcast limits how fast the brightness can change, to at most three flashes a second, and **Calm** halves that. Presets also aren't allowed to use LANcast's gold.
- **Presets work out their maths the way Winamp's MilkDrop does,** so they behave as their authors intended.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.92.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.91 if you need to. Presets you upload are kept in LANcast's data folder, not in the database, so a database backup doesn't include them.
