## Choose which libraries each person sees

- **Keep a library from someone.** In **Server › Users & access**, each member's row says what they can see, for example "Sees 2 of 5 libraries". **Libraries** opens a checklist. Every library is on by default, and one you add later is visible to everyone until you switch it off for someone.
- **Hidden means hidden everywhere.** The library leaves that person's sidebar, browsing, search, Home, Continue Watching, Memories, Year in Review, history and ratings. Opening a link to something in it says it doesn't exist. Administrators are never restricted.
- **Nothing is lost.** Their history in a hidden library is kept, and switching the library back on brings it all back.
- **The content rating limit reaches further.** It now also applies to Continue Watching and the review list, which it didn't before.

## Watchlist

- **Save things to watch later.** Every film and show has a **Watchlist** button beside Play, and an **Add to Watchlist** entry in More Options and in a poster's right-click menu.
- **A Watchlist shelf on Home**, newest first, appears once you've added something.
- **It tidies itself.** A film leaves your Watchlist when you finish it or mark it watched. A show leaves once you've watched its last episode.
- Your Watchlist is yours alone, like tags.

## Watch on your TV (DLNA)

- **Smart TVs, games consoles and VLC can now find LANcast and play from it**, with no LANcast app. Turn it on in **Server › DLNA**. It's off by default.
- **Choose who the TV watches as.** A TV sees exactly what that account sees. Pick a child's account, and every TV in the house gets that account's rating limit and libraries.
- **Files are played as they are**, so a TV plays what it can, and some TVs won't play every format. Nothing watched this way is added to anyone's history.
- **Windows needs two firewall rules** before a TV can find the server. LANcast doesn't change your firewall itself. The DLNA page gives the two commands to run in PowerShell as administrator, and shows why DLNA isn't running if something is missing.

## Network

- **Server › Network** says whether other devices on your network can reach the server, the addresses to use, and the security certificate's fingerprint, so you can check what a device asks you to trust.

## Fixes

- **Debug logging works for the installed server.** The switch in Troubleshooting never added detail to the server's log. It does now.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.84.exe`**.

This update changes the database, so 0.9.83 can't open it afterwards. Your existing backups still restore.
