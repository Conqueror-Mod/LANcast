## When a film ends

- **A film now ends on what to watch next.** If it's part of a collection, the first suggestion is the next film in it. The others are films like it from outside that collection, chosen by shared genres, director and lead actors. Nothing starts by itself: pick one, watch the film again, or close.
- **Resuming a collection carries on through it.** Pressing Resume on a collection's film in the spotlight, or Continue on a collection's page, queues the films after it in release order, the way a show plays its next episode. Before, the film played and then everything stopped. Films you've already watched are skipped.
- **An episode's own Continue carries on through its show** too, instead of stopping after one episode.

## Running as a service is your choice

- **Setup asks whether to install LANcast as a Windows service.** As a service, the server starts with Windows and stays available to your other devices whether or not anybody is signed in. Without one, the server runs while the LANcast app is open. The box is ticked by default, which is how LANcast has always installed. Silent installs take `/SERVICE` or `/NOSERVICE`.
- **You can switch later, in Settings › This app.** Administrators on the server's own computer can turn the service on or off there. LANcast copies your library to the right folder, checks the copy opens, and only then switches. The old copy is set aside, not deleted. Windows asks for permission once.

## Fixes

- **Update libraries from the tray no longer reloads an open LANcast.** It goes straight to Libraries and starts the scan, and anything playing keeps playing. The tray's other items, and opening LANcast a second time, behave the same way.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.94.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.93 if you need to.
