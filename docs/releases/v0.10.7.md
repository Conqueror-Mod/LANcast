## Audiobooks: a sleep timer, narrators and filters

- **A sleep timer.** Choose **Sleep** beside the speed in the book player: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or **End of chapter**. The time left shows beside it. Minutes count only while the book is playing, so pausing doesn't use them up. The last ten seconds fade out, then the book pauses and keeps your place.
- **End of chapter means the chapter you're in.** If you skip to another chapter, the timer follows you there, and it stops on the chapter's last second even at 2×.
- **Narrators.** LANcast reads who narrates a book from its files: a narrator tag where there is one, otherwise the composer tag, which is where most rippers put it. Every voice of a full-cast recording is listed. A book's page says "Read by …".
- **Filter by author, narrator and series** in an audiobooks library.
- **Your libraries are read once more for narrators.** The first scan of each audiobooks library after updating reads every book's tags again, so it takes longer than usual this one time. Narrators you've locked are left alone.

## Fixes

- **Continue Watching no longer empties after an evening of music.** Songs you stopped part-way through shared the shelf's places with your films, and enough of them pushed every film off. Films, music and audiobooks now each have their own Continue shelf, and they no longer crowd each other out.
- **A film watched together with a friend starts in seconds again.** It could take about eight minutes: the friend's server gave up waiting for the film after 30 seconds, while yours was still preparing it, and then asked again from the start. **Your friend's server needs this update too**, since the waiting happens there.
- **A friend's app now says which formats it can play.** Before, your server assumed the plainest browser and converted surround sound and HEVC pictures that your friend's app could have played as they are.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.7.exe`**.

This release doesn't change the database, so 0.10.6 can be reinstalled over it.
