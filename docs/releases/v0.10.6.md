## Play on another device

- **Send a film, episode or song from one of your devices to another.** Start something on your phone and send it to the desktop app by the TV, or pick a film on your phone and play it there without touching the PC. Both devices must be signed in to the same account; nobody else's devices are ever listed.
- **Move here** is the new cast button in the player. It sends what you're watching to the other device at the moment you're at, with the rest of your queue, and stops it here. A film that moves carries on: no pre-roll, no starting over.
- **Play on…** on a film's or an episode's page plays it on another device, starting wherever Play would start it there.
- **A remote for the other device.** **Devices**, in the sidebar or under **More** on a phone, lists your other devices and what each is playing, with pause and play, back 10 seconds, forward 30, previous, next, stop and volume.
- **The screen that receives says where it came from**: "Playing from" and the sending device's name, for a few seconds, even over a film playing full screen.
- **The desktop app offers itself by default.** A browser or a phone doesn't until you turn on **Settings › Player › Let my other devices play here**. **This device's name** is set there too, on every device, so the list and the notice say "Living room" rather than "Chrome on Windows".
- Audiobooks, games, comics, photos and live channels can't be sent yet.

## Fixes

- **A friend asking to join your Watch Together room now shows over a film.** When the desktop app was playing a film full screen, the request was hidden behind it, the one place it was needed.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.6.exe`**.

This release doesn't change the database, so 0.10.5 can be reinstalled over it.
