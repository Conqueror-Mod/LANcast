## Visualizers on phones

- **The visualizer choices fit on a phone now.** Cover, Spectrum, Scope, Trails, MilkDrop and the full-screen button sit under the visualizer, the way they do on a computer. Before, they ran off the right edge and covered the bottom of the picture.
- **MilkDrop says "Loading MilkDrop…" while it starts,** instead of showing an empty square. This matters most on a phone over mobile data, where it can take a moment. If it can't load, it tells you so and offers the album cover back.
- **Switching MilkDrop presets is quicker.** The presets either side of the one playing are fetched ahead of time.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.93.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.92 if you need to.
