## A new home page

The home page has been rebuilt around what you're in the middle of and what's new, and it now covers every kind of library, including audiobooks, comics and retro games.

- **Continue, in one place.** Films, shows, albums, audiobooks, comics and retro games you're partway through share one panel, most recent first. Each says where you are in its own terms: "1h 12m left", "S02E04 · next", "Track 6 of 11", "Page 41 of 120", "Saved yesterday". It holds five on a wide screen, four on a tablet and three on a phone, with at most two of any one kind. **See all** lists everything.
- **The spotlight and the panels share the first screen.** On a wide screen the Continue, Friends and On this day panels stand over the right of the spotlight. On a narrower screen they sit in a row beneath it, and on a phone Continue comes first. On a TV, Right from the spotlight's buttons takes you into Continue.
- **The spotlight's picture tints the background.** Its colours lean the background toward the picture's own, subtly.
- **Friends.** If you've paired with friends' servers, the Friends panel shows who's watching what, with Ask to join where they allow it. A server that can't be reached says so.
- **On this day** is now a panel beside Friends, and its photographs show the pictures. Before, the tiles showed only the filename.
- **New this week** replaces Recently Added, New Music and Recently Added Photos. It has one heading and a row for each kind that has something added in the last seven days. Nothing older is shown, so a quiet week has no section.
- **No empty boxes.** With nothing in progress, Continue lists your libraries instead. A brand-new server shows one welcome panel with a way to add a library.

## Changes you'll notice

- Continue Watching, Next up and Continue Listening are gone from Home. Everything they showed is in Continue.
- **Remove from Continue** is the one name for taking something off it, on Home and on a title's page. A comic offers **Mark as read** instead, and a retro game offers only **Go to details**.
- **Ask to join** can now be reached with a remote or arrow keys, on Home and on People.
- Films added on the same day as a batch of comics or games are no longer pushed out of the newest list by them.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.9.exe`**.

This release doesn't change the database, so 0.10.8 can be reinstalled over it.
