## Rate what you've watched

- **A new shelf on Home** lists the films you finished and the shows you watched in the last 30 days that you haven't rated yet.
- **Rate straight from the shelf.** Click a star under the poster and the title leaves the shelf. A show is rated once, as the show, not episode by episode.
- Half stars are still on each title's own page.

## Pick your account when you sign in

- **The sign-in page shows the accounts on your server**, so you can pick your name instead of typing it.
- **Only devices on your home network see the names.** From the internet, the sign-in page looks the same as before. The switch is in **Server › Users & access**.

## Passkeys in the desktop app

- **The LANcast window can now add and use passkeys**, with Windows Hello, when your server has a public certificate.
- The window now opens your server by its public name. It still connects straight to this computer, so nothing goes out to the internet.
- **The first time you open it after updating, you'll sign in once more**, and settings kept in the window, such as volume, start fresh once.

## Fixes

- **Next in a collection follows the film you just finished.** Watching one film out of order could make LANcast suggest a film further along the series instead of the next one.
- **A film no longer keeps playing out of reach.** If the window reloaded, restarted for an update, or moved to a new port while a film was in the mini-player, the film carried on with no way to stop it. It now stops.
- **No more white flash at startup.** The window opens on the screen it was on last time, instead of appearing briefly on your main screen first.
- **Pages left open across an update reload themselves** instead of going blank. This was most noticeable with a phone's home-screen shortcut.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.87.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.86 if you need to.
