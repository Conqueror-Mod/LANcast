## Watch Together keeps a friend in step on a converted film

When the host's film needs converting for a guest's browser (an AC3 soundtrack is the usual reason), the guest kept restarting. Every time the guest's player corrected its place to match the host, it asked the server for a fresh conversion, even when that part of the film was already converted. Each restart cost about 25 seconds, so the picture froze, jumped and reconnected over and over.

A correction inside what is already converted now just moves the guest's player there. The conversion runs once, and the guest stays in step with the host from then on.

## The audiobook sleep timer stops at the end of the chapter

"End of chapter" never stopped the book when the chapter was a file of its own, which most audiobooks split into. The timer ran out at the right moment, and the book then played on into the next chapter. It now stops there, with the next chapter loaded and ready, so pressing play carries on from where you'd expect.

The sleep timer's menu was also hard to read with a light Windows theme, which drew its choices white on white. They're drawn on the dark background now.

## Updating

Use the in-app update from 0.11.2. If you're on 0.11.1, the [0.11.2 notes](https://github.com/Conqueror-Mod/LANcast/releases/tag/v0.11.2) explain the one step that update may need. From anything older than 0.9.79, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.11.3.exe`**. There's no database change in this release (it stays at revision 97).
