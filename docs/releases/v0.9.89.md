## Now Playing

Playing music now opens a proper Now Playing screen instead of the cover with a toolbar over it.

- **The cover sits on the left, and what's playing is on the right**, in large type: the song, the artist, the album and the year. Click the album to go to it; the music keeps playing in the corner.
- **Queue and Lyrics are tabs beside the cover**, always on screen, instead of panels that covered the artwork when opened. Click any song in the queue to play it. The queue follows along, so the song that's playing stays at the top and you can see what's coming next.
- **The background takes its colour from the album cover**, softly blended into LANcast's own colours.
- **On a phone**, the cover is at the top with the details and tabs under it.
- The Lyrics and Queue buttons have left the control bar when music is playing, since both are on the screen now. Films keep their Queue button.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.89.exe`**.

This update doesn't change the database, so you can go back to 0.9.88 if you need to.
