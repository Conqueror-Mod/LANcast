## RetroAchievements on your profile

- **Connect your RetroAchievements account** on your **Profile** page with your username and Web API key, from your RetroAchievements settings page. LANcast checks them with RetroAchievements before keeping anything.
- **See your progress in LANcast:** your points, what you're playing now, your unlocks from the last two weeks, and how far through each game you are.
- **Only you see it.** Nobody else on your server, and no friend's server, can see your RetroAchievements.
- **Your key is kept encrypted**, with its own encryption key stored apart from the database, and it's never shown again after you connect. A backup restored on another computer asks you to connect again.
- **Nothing contacts RetroAchievements until you connect**, and after that only when you open your profile. Its pictures come through your server, so your devices never contact RetroAchievements directly.
- Earning achievements while playing in LANcast isn't here yet. This release shows the progress you've made elsewhere.

## More control over games

These are for games played in the LANcast desktop app.

- **The game menu (Escape) has pages:** a short main list, with **Picture**, **Speed & rewind** and **Controls** a step in. **Back**, or Escape, returns to the main list.
- **Fast-forward:** hold **Tab** or click the right stick, or turn it on in the menu. It runs at three times speed, without sound.
- **Rewind:** turn it on in the menu, then hold **R** or click the left stick to go back up to about 20 seconds. It's off unless you turn it on. Some N64 games can't rewind, and the menu says so.
- **Picture:** sharp or smooth pixels, whole-number scaling or fill the screen, the game's own shape, 4:3, square pixels or stretch, and scanlines for a CRT look. Your choices are remembered for each console.
- **Controls:** change which controller button or key does what, for each console. Each button shows the console's own name for it, for example "Z Trigger" on an N64. Escape and the controller's Guide button always open the menu. **Reset to defaults** puts everything back.

## View collection

- **More Options on a film now offers View collection** when the film is in a collection, on its own page and in the spotlight at the top of Home. Before, the only way in was the Collections button in a library.

## Fixes

- **Media keys work with keyboard software.** Play/Pause, Next and Previous did nothing when software such as iCUE or Logi Options+ was handling the keyboard. They work now.
- **Left and right seek after you've touched a slider.** After you used the volume slider or the seek bar, the arrow keys moved that slider instead of seeking. They seek now.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.88.exe`**.

**This update changes the database**, so 0.9.87 can't open it afterwards. Your existing backups still restore.
