## A new Settings

- **Settings is grouped four ways.** **Status** shows what the server is doing; **Server** is how it's set up; **Your account** follows you to every device; **This device** stays on this screen. Administrators land on the **Dashboard**. Old links and bookmarks still open the right place.
- **Scheduled tasks.** **Status › Scheduled tasks** lists everything the server does in the background: scanning, reading media files, fetching metadata, finding intros and credits, the TV guide, maintenance, update checks and more. Each shows when it last ran, whether that worked, when it's next due, and a **Run now** button.
- **Player settings before you press Play.** **This device › Player** holds the player's own preferences: quality on this device, night mode, dialogue boost and autoplay for films; night mode, equaliser, crossfade and fades for music. They're the same settings as the player's panel, so changing one changes the other.
- **Subtitle style follows you.** Set the colour, typeface, size and height of subtitles in **Your account › Languages & subtitles**, with a preview. Every device you sign in on draws them the same way. Until you choose a style, each device keeps its own.
- **Settings moved, not changed.** Transcoding, Quality, and Playback & progress are now their own panes. The public certificate lives in **Remote access**, and your account is split into **Profile**, **Security**, **Languages & subtitles** and **Privacy**. Every setting works as it did.

## Fixes and changes

- **Friends see posters.** People browsing a library you share with them saw only placeholder tiles. Your server now sends them the artwork.
- **A cleaner title page.** Play and the trailer stay in view; everything else (Remove from Continue Watching, Mark as watched, Add to playlist, Download, Pin to homepage, Remove) is behind **More Options**. The home spotlight's **More** is now **More Options** too.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.82.exe`**.
