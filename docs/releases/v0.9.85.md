## Optimized versions

- **Convert a title once, play it anywhere.** An optimized version is a copy of a film or episode, converted ahead of time into a format nearly every phone, browser and TV plays directly. When a device would otherwise need the original converted live, every time it's played, it plays the copy instead and starts at once.
- **You choose what gets one.** Administrators will find **Make optimized version** in a film or episode's More Options, and in a poster's right-click menu. On a show, season or collection, **Make optimized versions** does every episode or film in it.
- **The copy** is 1080p H.264 at about 8 Mbps with stereo sound. It's made in the background, one title at a time, with your graphics card when you have one. A 20-minute episode takes a minute or two. Making copies pauses while anyone is watching something being converted live.
- **Only when it helps.** A device that already plays the original directly keeps playing the original, which is the better file. Choosing a different audio track also plays the original.
- **Status › Conversions** shows the queue, how far the current copy has got, why it's paused if it is, and how much space copies take. You can try a failed copy again, cancel, or remove copies there.
- **Server › Transcoding** sets where copies are kept and how much space they may use (200 GB by default). The folder can't be inside a library. At the limit, new requests wait. Nothing is ever deleted to make room.
- Removing a title from the library removes its copy too.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.85.exe`**.

This update changes the database, so 0.9.84 can't open it afterwards. Your existing backups still restore.
