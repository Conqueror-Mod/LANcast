## Audiobooks

- **A new kind of library: Audiobooks.** Add a library and choose **Audiobooks**. It takes MP3, M4A and the other music formats, and `.m4b`, which a music library doesn't.
- **A folder of audio files is one book**, and its files are the book's parts, played in order. A file on its own at the top of the library, or beside folders of books, is a book by itself. Folders are never split up by guessing, so two separate recordings in one folder show as one book with two parts; put each in a folder of its own to make them two books.
- **Titles, series and numbers come from the folder names**, because audiobook files rarely carry them in their tags. `Deep Space Nine/03 - Warped - Abridged/` is *Warped*, Deep Space Nine #3, marked **Abridged**. The author comes from the files' album artist tag, else their artist tag. Covers come from a picture in the book's folder, whatever it's named.
- **Press Play and a book carries on where you left it**, five seconds back so you hear the last sentence again. **Start over** sits beside it.
- **A player bar along the bottom of the window** stays while you browse: back 15 seconds, forward 30, previous and next chapter, a scrubber over the whole book, and speed from 0.75× to 2×. Each book remembers its own speed; a book you've never played starts at 1×.
- **A book's page lists its chapters.** Choose one to play from there.
- **Each audiobooks library has its own Continue Listening shelf**, so a half-finished book doesn't sit among your films or push songs off the music shelf. A book counts as finished in its last minute.
- Audiobooks stay on this server: they aren't shared with paired servers or offered over DLNA. **Coming next**: a sleep timer, narrators, and filters by author, narrator and series.

## Albums identified with MusicBrainz

- **LANcast now identifies your albums with MusicBrainz**, in the background, one album at a time. Albums and artists it can't place with confidence go to **Review**, and **Fix match** now works on them: for an album, search for the right release or paste a MusicBrainz link; for an artist, choose from the artists your albums credit.
- **Your tags still win.** MusicBrainz only fills what your files leave empty: a missing year, missing genres, and a missing cover from the Cover Art Archive. A cover in your files or one you chose is never replaced.
- **Filter albums by type**: Album, EP, Single, Compilation, Live and the rest, on the Albums view of a music library. An album's page names its type when it isn't a plain album, and credits MusicBrainz with a link to the record.
- **This contacts musicbrainz.org and coverartarchive.org.** No key and no account; nothing is sent beyond the album's title, artist and year and LANcast's name and version. Turn it off in **Settings › Metadata › Identify albums with MusicBrainz**. The privacy policy has the details.
- **The first pass is slow on purpose.** MusicBrainz asks for one request a second, so a large music library takes a couple of hours to go through the first time. Everything plays as usual meanwhile.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.10.4.exe`**.

**This release changes the database**, so 0.10.3 can't be reinstalled over it afterwards. LANcast backs up the database before updating. Every music library is matched against MusicBrainz once after the update; albums you've locked are left alone.
