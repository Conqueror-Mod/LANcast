## Collections continue like shows

- **A collection picks up where you left off.** A collection you're partway through leads with the next film, named: **Up next: …**, or **Continue: …** when that film is already started. It moves forward through the films in release order, the way a show moves through its episodes.
- **The next film goes on Continue Watching.** Finish a film in a collection and the next one appears there. **Remove from Continue Watching** takes it off, and it comes back once you finish another film in that collection.
- **Better "Because you watched".** In the middle of a collection, the home spotlight suggests its next film ("Because you watched *the previous one*") instead of something that only shares a genre.

## Fixes

- **Episodes no longer finish as they start.** When an episode ended and the next one began, the new episode could be recorded as watched within seconds: a checkmark in the episode list and an entry in your history. Only the episode you actually finished is marked now.
- **Play from start starts from the start.** On a film's page it could resume instead, on accounts with a preferred audio language.
- **Leaving the player leaves fullscreen.** Backing out of a fullscreen film to Home left the window with no title bar and no way out of fullscreen. Any way out of the player now ends fullscreen.
- **Closing the window during a film stops the film.** ✕ puts LANcast in the tray. A film used to keep playing in the hidden window; now it stops and keeps your place. Music keeps playing, since listening with the window away is what the tray is for.
- **Music is listened to.** Music now says **Continue listening**, **Because you listened to** and **Played N times**. A long run of tracks that needed converting no longer stops every few songs to ask whether you're still watching.

## About LANcast

- **A new About tab.** Settings › About shows the version, the licence, everything LANcast is built from, and the notes for the version you're running, these included. It's built into the app and asks no other server, and it's open to every account. The tray's **About LANcast…** opens it.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 the in-app update works as usual. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.80.exe`**.
