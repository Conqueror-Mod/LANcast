## Music

- **Even out loudness.** Shuffle a 1970s record next to a modern one and you no longer reach for the volume every few songs. LANcast uses the ReplayGain measurements most taggers already write into your files (foobar2000, Picard, beets, Mp3tag), and Opus files' own loudness tags. Turn it on in **Settings › Player › Music › Even out loudness**, or in the player's settings while music plays:
  - **By track** evens out everything, which suits a shuffled mix.
  - **By album** keeps an album's own quiet and loud songs as they were mastered.
- **It only ever turns music down**, never past a track's own peak, so nothing distorts. That's why it's off by default: switched on, most modern music plays quieter, and you turn the volume up once. A track without the tags plays as it is. Gapless albums and crossfades keep each track at its own level.

## Comics

- **`.cb7` comics are read** as well as `.cbz` and `.pdf`. Each page appears in about a tenth of a second, even deep into a long issue. The first jump to the last page of an issue nobody has opened takes a few seconds, once.
- **A comic no longer gets stuck in Review.** Editing one, for example setting its rating, used to put it there with nothing to fix it. The same was true of photos, music and games, which can't be matched to anything either.

## Pictures

- **RAW photos from your camera are shown** (`.cr2`, `.cr3`, `.nef`, `.arw`, `.dng`, `.raf`, `.orf`, `.rw2` and more), using the full-size picture the camera stores inside every RAW file. Nothing is developed or changed.
- **A RAW kept beside a JPEG of the same name shows once**, as the JPEG, the way cameras set to RAW+JPEG save them.

## Dashboard

- **Media sent.** A new chart shows how much your server streams, in Mbit/s: films, music, downloads and conversions, for you and for friends. Pages and pictures aren't counted.
- **The charts move while you watch them.** Each one updates every few seconds, including the minute in progress. Before, they looked frozen until the page was reloaded.

## Report an issue

- **Report an issue is a form in the app.** Say what happened, and press **Send**. It no longer opens a mail program, which in the desktop window showed Windows' "pick an app" box and then went nowhere.
- **What goes with it is written next to the button:** your LANcast version, its operating system, which app you used, and whether you're an administrator. Never your name or address unless you type it. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **An administrator can attach the server log**, and read every line of it first. Members can't, because the log names the server's folders, addresses and accounts.

## Fixes

- **No more console windows flashing up** while LANcast looks for credits and intros, or recognises faces, when the server runs as an app rather than a service.
- **The credits detection setting says what it does now**: it drives Skip intro, Skip credits and the Up next card.

## Updating

From 0.9.79 or later, use the in-app update. From anything older, run **`LANcast-Setup-0.9.99.exe`**.

**This update changes the database.** Once it has run, 0.9.98 and earlier can't open your library. LANcast backs the database up first ("Before an update" in Backup & storage), so going back to 0.9.98 means reinstalling it and restoring that backup with its `restore` command.

**The first scan of each music library after updating reads every track's tags once**, to find their loudness: about a minute and a half for 10,000 tracks. Scans after that are as quick as before.
