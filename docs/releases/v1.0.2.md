## LANcast 1.0.2

Collections now reach the friends you share with, and you can let a friend download from a library you share.

## New

- **Allow downloads, per share.** A shared library used to be for watching only, and a friend's Downloads page had nothing to fill it. Each library you share with a paired server now has an **Allow downloads** switch, under its age limit (People, then **Choose what they can see** under the server). It starts off, and every share you already have stays off until you turn it on. With it on, your friend gets a **Download** button on your titles and the original file, never a re-encoded copy. The switch never reaches further than watching does: the same library and the same age limit apply, and being in a Watch Together room with somebody does not let them keep the film. Downloads from a friend's server show in your Downloads list with the rest, and open on their server.
- **TVs can pair with a code.** Settings, Security has a **Pair a device** button. A TV that shows a code signs in there, and its key is listed below, where revoking it signs the TV out. This is the groundwork for the LANcast TV app, which is not available yet.

## Fixed

- **Your collections show up for friends.** A friend browsing a library you share saw every film but none of your collections, because nothing on their screen could open one. Collections now appear and open to their films, oldest first. A collection that takes in films from a library you did not share lists only the films you did, and films above the share's age limit stay hidden.

## Updating

Use the in-app update (Settings, General), or run `LANcast-Setup-1.0.2.exe` over the top. **For a friend to download from you, both servers need 1.0.2:** yours to send the file, theirs to show the button.

This release moves the database to schema 98, which cannot be undone. Once 1.0.2 has run, 1.0.1 will refuse to open the database, so going back means restoring a backup taken before the update. LANcast takes one automatically before an in-app update.
