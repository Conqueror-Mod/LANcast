## LANcast 1.0.6

A season keeps playing, and the player's keys stay awake.

## Fixed

- **A season finale rolls on.** Playing an episode from a season's page now queues the rest of the show, not just that season, so the last episode of a season moves on to the first of the next. The show's own **Continue** already worked this way.
- **Up Next on every episode.** The Up Next card used to need LANcast to have found an episode's end credits, so episodes where it hadn't never offered what came next. Those episodes now get the card for their last 13 seconds, timed so its countdown runs out just as the episode would end. Episodes whose credits were found keep the card at the credits, along with Skip credits.
- **The arrow keys keep working after the controls fade.** In the desktop app, a few seconds into a film the controls fade, and after that the arrow keys (seek and volume), Enter and Escape did nothing; letters and Space still worked. They work again.
- **One Escape is enough.** Pressing Escape after the controls had faded only brought them back, so leaving fullscreen or the player took a second press. It now does what it says the first time.

## Also

- The installer now places `NOTICE` and `COMMERCIAL.md` beside `LICENSE`, and `NOTICE` lists every bundled component.

## Updating

Use the in-app update (Settings, General), or run `LANcast-Setup-1.0.6.exe` over the top. The database is unchanged since 1.0.2, so going back to 1.0.5 is just reinstalling it.
